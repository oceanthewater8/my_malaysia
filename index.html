Malaysia's best tourism spots

Posted 20th February 2025 by Ocean

Batu Caves

Batu Caves is a series of limestone caves and temples in Gombak, Malaysia, north of Kuala Lumpur. It's a popular tourist destination and a place of pilgrimage for Tamil Hindus.

Gunung Kinabalu

Gunung Kinabalu (Mount Kinabalu) is the tallest mountain in Borneo and Malaysia, and is one of the most accessible peaks in the world and it is one of the best tourism spots in Malaysia!

Trying diffrent foods in Malaysia

Posted 20th February 2025 by Ocean

Nasi Lemak

Nasi lemak is a fragrant, coconut milk-rich rice dish that's commonly eaten for breakfast in Malaysia. The name comes from the Malay words nasi (rice) and lemak (fat or rich) and it is also one of my favourite foods too!

Ais Kacang

Ais kacang, also known as ABC, is a Malaysian dessert of shaved ice, beans, jelly, and sweet syrup. It's a popular treat in Malaysia, Singapore, and Brunei.

Laksa

Laksa is a spicy noodle soup that's popular in Southeast Asia. It's made with a variety of noodles, typically rice noodles, and often includes meat, seafood, or vegetables. The soup is usually made with a coconut curry broth or a broth seasoned with tamarind or asam gelugur.

Malaysia's traditions

Posted 20th February 2025 by Ocean

Greetings

A bow is a common greeting, especially when meeting someone for the first time and light handshake is acceptable if someone extends their hand, greeting older people first is the most important in all curcumstances.

Gift giving

It's customary to give a gift, known as buah tangan, or fruit of the hands and it is also considered polite to initially refuse a gift. It's also customary to not open the gift in front of the giver and it is recommended to bring chocolate or pastries when invited home to be polite when we enter other peoples home.

Performing Arts

Each ethnic group has distinct performing arts like Woodcarving, woodcarving is a traditional art form that uses carved wood to ornament doors and window panels and also Mak yong, Mak yong is a form of art that incorporates dance and drama and for me i think that performing arts is a lot of fun!
